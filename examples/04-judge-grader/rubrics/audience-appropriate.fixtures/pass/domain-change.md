Great question — this is totally normal after switching your domain to us.
Think of your domain name like a person's name, and there's a giant address
book on the internet that looks up what "address" (computer) that name
points to. When you point your domain to us, it takes a little while — often
a few hours, sometimes up to a day — for every copy of that address book
around the world to update with your new address. Until they all catch up,
some visitors might still land on your old site. There's nothing broken and
nothing you need to redo; just give it up to 24 hours and it should sort
itself out everywhere.
