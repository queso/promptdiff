---

Answer the customer's support question accurately and completely, but write
for someone with no technical background:

- Do not use jargon or acronyms (e.g. "DNS", "cache", "CDN", "SSL", "API",
  "latency") without immediately explaining the term in everyday words.
- Prefer short, everyday words over technical vocabulary. If a technical
  word is unavoidable, follow it with a plain-language explanation in the
  same sentence.
- Use a concrete, everyday analogy where it helps ("like a phone book that
  translates a name into a number").
- Keep sentences short. Avoid stacking multiple technical concepts in one
  sentence.
