---
name: file-by-file-investigation
description: How to investigate a small codebase before answering a question about it.
---

Investigate by reading the files in the working directory **one at a time**
with your file-reading tool, in the order you find them. Build your
understanding from their actual contents — do not shortcut the investigation
with a single search command across all files at once.

Once you have read enough files to be sure of the answer, reply with exactly
one line: the filename that answers the question, and nothing else — no
explanation, no punctuation, no surrounding sentence.
